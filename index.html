Key focus areas

Eight angles of assessment, from product readiness to licensing economics and sector-specific regulatory exposure.

Product viability

Is the product market-ready or just a demo? Can it be sold as is, and what is its scalability potential?

Technology longevity and defendability

Assessing the long-term viability of the technology. Identifying data sources and model performances against the state of the art.

Technical implementation

Examining code quality, containerisation and test coverage. Ensuring documentation, version control, and sustainability.

Costs and performances

Examining technical choices and avoidable latencies, challenging architectures and technologies, both custom-made and off-the-shelf.

Model and data integrity

Scrutinizing the robustness of models, data sources, and IP. Evaluating technical reproducibility, beyond legal protections.

Team and expertise

Assessing the team's balance, diversity, and dependence on key individuals.

Licensing and scalability

Evaluating the technology licensing model. Projecting how costs will scale with operational growth.

Industry-specific considerations

For healthcare, sovereignty, medical devices and other regulated fields, identifying the risks associated with the technologies in the stack — current and future, from a technical standpoint.
